Privacy policy — gog-jeanchouky

gog-jeanchouky is a private, single-user application. It accesses only the Google account that authorised it, and no other person's data.

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
